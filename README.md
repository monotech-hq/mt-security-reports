# Security reports

Where to report a security issue in anything we publish, privately.
Nothing else lives here: no code, no public issues, no discussion.

## How to report

Open the **Security** tab of this repository and choose **Report a
vulnerability**. What you write there is seen by us alone.

Tell us what you found, where, and how to reproduce it. A proof of
concept helps; a working exploit is not needed. If the issue is in a
specific project or version, name it.

Please do not open a public issue, and do not test against systems or
data that are not yours.

## What we do

- We read every report and answer as soon as we can. We cannot
  promise a fixed time.
- We keep you informed while we investigate and fix.
- We fix confirmed issues as quickly as their severity warrants, and we
  tell you when the fix has shipped.
- We credit you in the published advisory if you want us to. Say so in
  the report, along with the name to use.

## Good faith

We will not take action against anyone who reports in good faith,
keeps the details private until a fix has shipped, and avoids harming
people or data along the way.
